On Monday, Sticky was spotted visiting East Westford Coffee. For those in the know, East Westford is known for dark roasts, a cozy atmosphere and the insane ramblings of its manager. Sticky spent several hours in the establishment, which speaks highly of the quality of both product and clientele. Indeed, ask anyone who’s ever been. They’ll surely recall many a cozy afternoon there, enjoying both the drinks and the sight of the manager licking the wallpaper, stopping only to swear he once dropkicked one of the Kardashians.

On Wednesday, Sticky was photographed enjoying a drink at Café Du Bootsy, a trendy establishment uptown. While our canine friend seemed to stick to the coffee, Café Du Bootsy offers an extensive menu, including French toast sticks, chicken nuggets and artisanal Sunny D.

On Thursday, a fan caught up to Sticky at Beachin’ Blends in the early afternoon. Apparently Sticky had spent all morning in the touristy spot and even posed for photos with numerous families. Unfortunately the fan was unable to learn Sticky’s sugar packet of choice, due to being kicked out for starting a fight with the Coca Cola machine.

Dinkle Has Spoken on the Peepy Situation


Dinkle has been raising awareness for the Peepies and Ouiouis that disappeared last night using his Dinkletoob channel. Videos titled “Dinkle’s Statement” and “THEY’RE ALL GONE!?” have gone viral, gaining over 1.2 million views in just a few short hours, despite not being a reputable news source.


“I’m freaking out, guys!” Dinkle yells in the video THEY’RE ALL GONE!?. “I don’t know what to do. I loved each and every one of them so much. Ouiouis and Peepies were what made me a celebrity in the first place and who am I without their support? I hope they’re okay and they’re not being like, tortured or something. That would make me really sad because probably some of them haven’t subscribed yet and they haven’t heard my bell ring!”


Dinkle went famous many years ago, starring in cult classic shows like Baby Dingle and Ring Dingle’s Bell. He has pledged to use his popularity to aid in the search for the missing residents by ringing his bell every hour, on the hour. “Every time I ring this bell, I remember another one of my friends who went missing. So please, like and subscribe to provide support to those who are out searching! Oh wait, what’s that in the sk-”


His stream abruptly paused, but resumed shortly thereafter, gaining over a thousand new viewers during that short pause. Despite the police force having received zero funds from Dinkle’s stream, the crowd support has shown increased numbers.
